BSFix is a youth-led climate project from Sri Lanka. Over the next two years we will build compact units that use Black Soldier Fly larvae to turn food waste into compost and protein-rich larvae, right where the waste is produced.

From December 2026 to June 2027 we are designing and building prototype units with local fabricators. In mid-2027 we run our first pilot, and in 2028 we take the units to public sites in and around Colombo.

If your organisation produces food waste and would like to host a unit, [tell us about your site](../../host-a-unit/).
